SOC · Changi One lunch at a time

Come for the food, stay for the stories.

Applied Foodies is our lunch-hour quest across the east side. We invite colleagues from every corner of Applied Materials, let them choose their favourite makan spot near the SOC, and spend the meal trading chip-industry secrets, career advice and the stories behind the badge. Then we score the food out of 10, snap a photo, and pin it on the map. Every pin is a new friend.
